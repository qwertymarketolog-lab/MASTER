# MASTER — Roadmap v1.0

**Status:** staged proposal; dates and targets are not commitments.

## Stage 0 — Foundation (current)
- Review and approve product scope, legal model, data model, architecture, security, payment model, and change governance.
- Build requirements traceability and risk register.
- Identify provider options and verify official API/contract availability.
- Define pilot acceptance criteria and operating ownership.
**Exit:** no unresolved P0 contradiction; legal and payment launch gates documented.

## Stage 1 — Lesnoy pilot MVP
- PWA customer and provider journeys.
- Limited home-service categories.
- Profiles, requests, offers, agreement snapshots, order lifecycle, change requests.
- Secure messaging/notifications through verified available channels.
- Evidence upload and completion/acceptance workflow.
- Basic trust signals and support/complaint intake.
- Admin tools with audited least-privilege access.
**Exit:** end-to-end tested orders; support, incident, privacy, payment and legal procedures ready; pilot metrics observable.

## Stage 2 — Trust and expert review
- Structured complaint case.
- Expert directory and eligibility.
- Three-expert selection, conflict checks, recusal, separate opinions, majority rationale, INCONCLUSIVE.
- Expert compensation and audit.
**Exit:** independent legal/operational review and controlled pilot of advisory opinions.

## Stage 3 — Professional services
- IT, design, marketing, accounting and eligible legal/professional categories.
- Category-specific briefs, deliverables, IP/confidentiality terms, acceptance checklists.
**Exit:** category legal and workflow packages approved individually.

## Stage 4 — Projects and teams
- Multi-order projects, tasks, dependencies, project roles, budgets, milestones.
- Separate provider agreements and scoped access.
**Exit:** project liability, IP, payment, security and support models approved.

## Stage 5 — Materials, logistics, AI and expansion
- Supplier and delivery integrations.
- AI-assisted classification and matching with human override.
- Additional cities only after local supply/demand and operating readiness are demonstrated.

## Pilot metrics (targets to validate, not existing results)
Track qualified providers, active providers, customer requests, response time, order conversion, completed orders, cancellation, complaint rate, repeat usage, verified evidence completeness, provider retention, and contribution margin. Do not optimize for raw registrations alone.
