# MASTER — Business and Legal Model v1.0

**Status:** business hypothesis and legal questions for professional review; not a legal opinion.

## 1. Intended model
MASTER is intended to operate as a digital intermediary/organizer that connects customers with independent service providers and supplies workflow, communications, evidence, and complaint-support tools. The actual legal classification depends on product behavior and contracts, not the label used in marketing.

## 2. Parties and responsibilities
- Customer: describes the need, supplies accurate information, agrees terms, provides access, pays according to the contract, and reviews deliverables.
- Provider: confirms eligibility, makes an offer, independently performs the service, observes applicable professional/tax rules, and is responsible for its contractual obligations.
- MASTER: provides platform functionality and performs only the coordination/verification/support obligations expressly undertaken in its contracts and applicable law.
- Experts: provide independent professional opinions within competence and disclosed scope.

## 3. No blanket disclaimer
Terms cannot eliminate mandatory statutory rights or liability for MASTER's own actions/omissions. Avoid claims such as “MASTER is never responsible” or “all responsibility belongs to the provider.” Allocate responsibility accurately for matching, verification representations, payment handling, communications, data protection, complaints, and platform operations.

## 4. Provider eligibility
Support separate statuses for self-employed persons using NPD, individual entrepreneurs, and legal entities. NPD is not itself a legal entity and may be used by eligible individuals and eligible IPs. Validate current status through lawful, official mechanisms at appropriate times. Category-specific restrictions, licenses, permits, qualifications, employee/subcontractor rules, and prohibited activities require separate checks. Do not infer eligibility solely from self-declaration.

## 5. Customer and provider contracts
Determine whether the service contract is directly between customer and provider, whether MASTER is an agent or otherwise involved, and which party issues receipts, handles refunds, warranties, and complaints. Terms, UI, payment flow, and actual conduct must all match the reviewed legal model.

## 6. Platform economy legislation
Assess Federal Law 289-FZ and related rules against actual functionality, including order placement, provider admission, ranking, transaction participation, payments, complaints, and information exchange. Do not assume that calling MASTER a “listing site” or “technical intermediary” excludes statutory duties.

## 7. Consumer and category rules
Review consumer-protection duties, information disclosure, remote contracting, cancellation/refund rights, warranties, and special rules for construction, regulated trades, legal services, accounting, financial advice, and other professional categories. Some legal services may require a particular professional status or confidentiality safeguards.

## 8. Expert panel
The panel is a private expert-review mechanism, not a court or statutory arbitration tribunal. Its conclusions are advisory unless a separately reviewed, lawful agreement gives them a defined contractual effect. Preserve access to state courts and mandatory consumer remedies. Expert selection must ensure competence, independence, conflict disclosure, recusal, equal submissions, reasoned opinions, and an inconclusive outcome.

## 9. Intellectual property and confidentiality
For IT/design/engineering projects, define ownership or license scope, transfer timing, source files, third-party/open-source components, acceptance, confidentiality, personal data access, and portfolio permissions before work begins. Do not assume payment automatically transfers all IP rights.

## 10. Required legal deliverables before pilot
- Written legal classification and role map.
- Customer terms and provider terms.
- Privacy policy/notices and data-processing agreements as applicable.
- Payment, agency, fiscal receipt, refund, and commission analysis.
- Complaint and expert-review rules.
- Category eligibility matrix.
- Consumer disclosures and cancellation/warranty rules.
- Vendor contracts and cross-border data-flow review.
- 289-FZ applicability memo and required operating procedures.

## 11. Decision gate
No public launch or live money movement until qualified counsel and the selected payment provider confirm the actual contractual and fiscal flow. Legal unknowns are launch blockers, not assumptions.
